## SUBJECT

The entire video features only one young Asian male creator.

He has short black center-parted hair, black rectangular glasses, a clean natural appearance, and wears a plain black T-shirt with a black utility vest.

Maintain the exact same identity, face, hairstyle, glasses, body proportions, and outfit throughout the entire video.

The scene takes place inside the same warm-toned retro personal creative studio / home office.

The creator sits behind a dark brown wooden desk. On the desk are a cream-colored retro mechanical keyboard, mouse, vintage CRT monitor, warm desk lamp, telephone, transparent storage boxes, and small creative-work accessories.

The background contains wooden cabinets, venetian blinds, documents, shelves, and warm practical lighting.

The core visual concept:

The creator's real physical workspace gradually transforms into a physical post-production environment.

Instead of controlling editing software only through a computer, the creator begins directly manipulating color-grading curves, parameter panels, preview monitors, and editing interfaces floating in the real world.

Professional post-production interfaces physically exist inside the room, with correct perspective, depth, reflections, occlusion, depth of field, and camera parallax.

The entire sequence should feel like one continuous transformation rather than a collection of unrelated scenes.

Use character movement, hand gestures, the CRT monitor, foreground objects, and dynamic camera movement to create seamless visual transitions.
