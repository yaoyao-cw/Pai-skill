# Named generated-design failures

Name the pattern. Agents avoid patterns they can recognize. When reviewing output, point at the name, then write the observable replacement.

## Layout defaults

**Generic hero plus card grid**
Centered headline, three feature cards, soft gradient. Replacement — first viewport is the argument. Cards only if the items are true peers and the job is comparison of those peers.

**Generic SaaS dashboard**
Equal KPI tiles, sidebar chrome, leftover chart. Replacement — one focal relationship. Rank unequal findings. Do not force four metrics into four identical cells.

**Identical section silhouettes**
Every section is heading + two columns + three cards. Replacement — vary density and geometry with the reader question. Keep one visual grammar, not one section template.

**Underfilled split**
Two-column row with a short left rail and a large empty rectangle. Replacement — reflow, rebalance, or stack. Open space must amplify a focal object.

**Narrow table in a wide section**
Evidence squeezed to prose measure while columns sit empty. Replacement — tables, charts, calculators, and comparisons use the full evidence width of the section.

**Crushed table**
Wide table forced into broken words or unreadable wrap. Replacement — allow horizontal scroll with an overflow cue, stack on mobile, or cut columns rather than shrink type.

## Hierarchy repairs that are not hierarchy

**Cards in cards / borders as structure**
Boxes used because grouping was never designed. Replacement — proximity, type role, and shared alignment. Borders only when they encode a real surface (callout, contrast band, control).

**Pills for ordinary metadata**
Date, customer, period, status wrapped in capsules. Replacement — metadata type role, sentence case, no badge unless the state is exceptional and sourced.

**Dark rounded frame around every chart**
A device bezel used as decoration. Replacement — chart sits on the page surface. Axis, direct labels, and caption do the work.

**Icon tiles**
Colored squares with mixed stroke icons. Replacement — no decorative icons. If a control needs a symbol, use one family at one size, never as section identity.

## Typography and copy tells

**Tracked eyebrows and kickers**
ALL CAPS overlines, "01 — Overview", decorative numbered labels. Replacement — a real heading that states the reader question.

**Em dash clutter**
UI copy stitched with em dashes. Replacement — period, colon, or a shorter sentence.

**Tiny muted prose + invented sizes**
Body at 13px gray on gray, one-off 27px titles. Replacement — published type roles only. Peers share role, size, weight, line-height, and numeric treatment.

**Authoring vocabulary leak**
Page copy says "this composition foregrounds the focal relationship." Replacement — write for the stakeholder. Never mention the method.

**Process narration**
"We grouped the metrics into three themes." Replacement — show the themes. Delete the diary.

**Repeated conclusion stack**
Recommendation, then summary, then rationale, then takeaways, all restating one sentence. Replacement — one evidence home. Later sections add new questions (assumptions, method, sources).

## Color and motion tells

**Color as applause**
Green because a number is favorable. Replacement — monochrome by default. Color only for state, action, or a labelled series, with a non-color cue.

**Decorative atmosphere**
Gradients, glows, blobs, glass, grain, stripes, fake depth, grid wallpaper. Replacement — none. A gradient is allowed only as a labelled continuous data scale.

**Motion as legitimacy**
Marquees, typing cursors, pulsing live dots, scroll-reveal of every block, parallax. Replacement — static first paint. Motion only for a control the reader is operating.

**Stock and synthetic spectacle**
AI illustrations, abstract 3D shapes, fake product shots, mandatory hero media. Replacement — the evidence is the media. If there is no image in the source, do not invent one.

## Data tells

**Bars because numbers exist**
A bar chart of four unrelated KPIs. Replacement — a sentence, a table, or one shared-scale comparison.

**Bars that do not share a scale**
Full-width tracks with incomparable maxima. Replacement — one scale, or do not use length.

**Legend instead of direct labels**
Color key in a corner, anonymous lines. Replacement — label the series on the plot.

**Numerator compared without base**
"120 incidents vs 40 incidents" across unequal populations. Replacement — rates, or state the bases in the same row.

**Hand-picked audit rows**
A "key findings" table of only flattering records. Replacement — a stated filter (all failures, all exceptions, every row named in the decision) plus a way to inspect all rows.

## Brand-surface tells

**Looks like product UI**
App sidebar, command palette chrome, dashboard density on an editorial report.

**Looks like a campaign**
Hero slogan, lifestyle art, pricing-card theater on a decision brief.

**Looks like a template**
Every artifact of a category shares the same skeleton regardless of evidence shape.

Replacement — official authorship through shell, type, grid, and restraint. Structure follows the reader's job.
