# Fusion pilot review

Creative call: three five-second reads, rather than the original four scene montage. Clay character acting initiates the paper shop reveal. A single bread cluster travels from oven to one serving saucer and into branded packaging. The bag front, back and characters have explicit occlusion order. The first actor has a newly generated 16-pose pull, rather than a single cutout arm rotation.

Borrowed the conceptual workflow of gbro-collage-broll, not its model execution. Used built-in image generation for reference-guided pull poses and a new original bag cutout; local deterministic composition for the film. Registry was searched and paper-collage-reveal examined; generic scattering was rejected in favor of three panels from one shop cutout opening in a causal sequence.

Measured: final full check passes lint/runtime/layout/contrast/craft/audio with zero errors and warnings. Motion assertions are not enabled in this project; zero reported motion issues alone is not a movement validation. New actor sheet is 8192×512, 16 poses. Original accepted toast, coffee and croissant cycles are retained. Final video streams/duration/audio levels are checked separately in the output validation record.

Looked at: 16-pose pull sheet; complete scene/phone review and transition strips; close samples of pull timing, the 9.7–10.85s bread transition and packaging occlusion; final brand hold. Changed the phantom crossing seam to a single paper strip; moved chef away from the plate; separated bag front/back entrance from falling bread after discovering a clipped bread edge; hid actors during initial ascent to prevent their feet showing below bag.

Weakest beat: first scene's grip-to-strip connection is approximate, not a physically constrained rig. The bread uses a playful toss and match cut, not a continuous spatial camera. The final brand holds still after the characters finish their gestures. All are deliberate pilot choices; the user accepted this exact fusion pilot and requested publication.
