# Exploded view

**Answers:** What is inside, layer by layer.

**Example:** `assets/examples/under.js`: (Under your street)

## When to use it

The question is what is inside or underneath: a street, a cake, a riverbank, a building, a beehive.

## Layout

The thing is cut into layers that lift apart vertically, each a slab drawn in its real material, labelled in the margin with its depth or size. A press moves the camera or a marker down one layer; the layers stay apart so all are seen at once.

## The statechart it suits

A chain going down (or in): street → services → … each transition labelled with how far down it is. Rules: you reach a layer only through the ones above it.

## Buttons

Next step goes down one layer; a second button goes back up; Play all drops all the way and returns.
